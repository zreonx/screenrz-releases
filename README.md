## ✨ Features

- **Screen, Window & Display Capture**: Record entire monitors or specific application windows at up to 60 FPS in high-definition.
- **Webcam Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Real-time camera overlay with draggable placement, customizable shapes (circle, rounded squircle), and horizontal mirroring.
- **Multi-Format Export**: Choose your preferred output format:
  - **MP4 (`video/mp4`)**: Universally compatible with all video editors, mobile devices, and media players.
  - **WebM (`video/webm`)**: Lightweight, efficient VP8/VP9 capture.
- **Crystal-Clear Audio Mixing**: Simultaneous capture of system audio (desktop loopback) and microphone input.
- **Compact Floating Controls & Tray Integration**: Minimized controls that stay out of your way while recording, with a live system tray indicator.
- **Built-in Video Player & Management**: Review, trim, seek, and manage your recordings with instant playback.

## 💻 System Requirements

- **Operating System**: Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11
- **Processor**: Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 or higher
- **Memory**: 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended for 1080p 60fps)
- **Disk Space**: ~250 MB free space
